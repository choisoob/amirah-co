Chapter 01

Before the magic…

The Ordinary World

✦

Some stories begin in castles. Others begin somewhere much quieter — a room above a bakery, one candle burning low, rain against the glass, and a box of letters that never found their owners.

That is where the catalogue started.

Chapter 02

The world you knew was never the whole story.

The Invitation

Amirah & Co. — workshop no. 3

Welcome to the workshop. The third stair complains; mind it. Your artifact is catalogued, sealed and waiting.

It arrives without a postmark, sealed in oxblood wax struck from the original die. Inside: the artifact's name, its maker, and the date it was finished. Read it before you open anything else.

The Journey

✦

Wood is cut in winter and rested a full year before it is turned. Wool is milled in the north. The lanterns are lit because nothing here is rushed to meet a season.

Beneath the shop floor, the archive: shelves that run further than the candlelight reaches. Instruments as much as garments — astrolabes, seals, ledgers. Pieces built to be kept and handed on.
